Common mistakes (and how to avoid them)

Writing like a blog post
Write for spoken delivery: short sentences, breathing room, punctuation.

Unreviewed pronunciations
Add pronunciation hints and verify sensitive passages before export.

Cloning without permission
Get explicit, documented consent. This is non-negotiable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free plan?
ElevenLabs usually offers a free plan or trial with limits. Quotas evolve over time, so check the official website.
How does voice cloning work?
You provide an audio sample and the tool learns voice characteristics. Only use voice cloning with explicit permission.
Does ElevenLabs create videos?
It primarily generates audio (voiceovers). Video is built in your editing software where you sync narration, add visuals and captions.
Can I use it commercially?
It depends on your plan and licensing terms. Read the official terms before publishing ads, audiobooks, etc.
How do I improve quality?
Write for speech, split scripts into segments, use punctuation to guide intonation, and review pronunciations before exporting.
Which languages are available?
Language coverage changes. Test your target language and accent on a short excerpt before producing at scale.

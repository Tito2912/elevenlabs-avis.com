Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pick a plan?

Start by estimating usage (minutes/month) and your use cases (voiceovers, long narration, API). Pricing and quotas change, so always confirm on the official pricing page before deciding.

Can I clone a voice?

Only with explicit consent (or the required rights). Keep a human review step and avoid deceptive uses (deepfakes).

What audio format is best for video?

For editing, prefer a clean WAV (or a high-quality MP3), with stable levels and no clipping. Use the blog’s QA checklist before publishing.

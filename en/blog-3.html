Voice cloning: safety, rights and best practices

Voice cloning can save time, but it raises major questions around rights and trust. Here’s a practical checklist to use ElevenLabs (or any AI voice tool) responsibly and ship credible voiceovers.

Advertisement

1) Consent & rights: the golden rule

  • Explicit consent. Never clone a voice without clear, traceable permission.
  • No impersonation. Avoid content that could mislead people about someone’s identity.
  • Licensing. Check what your plan allows (commercial use, distribution, restrictions).
  • Transparency. Disclose synthetic voice use when context requires it (ads, customer support, etc.).

2) Quality checklist for credible voiceovers

  1. Write for speech: short sentences, breathing room, punctuation.
  2. Generate in segments and iterate on problematic parts.
  3. Fix proper nouns and acronyms (pronunciation).
  4. Review on multiple outputs (headphones + speakers) before publishing.

3) Privacy basics (for teams)

  • Minimize data sent to the platform (avoid sensitive information in scripts).
  • Document access to accounts, API keys and exported audio files.
  • If you clone a voice, keep proof of consent.

4) Keep a simple consent record (template)

Not legal advice. If you use voice cloning, keep a short “consent record” you can reference internally:

  • Who: voice owner name + contact.
  • Scope: allowed uses (channels, languages, formats).
  • Duration: start/end date + how to revoke consent.
  • Disclosure: when you must label synthetic audio.
  • Storage: where samples/models are stored + who has access.

5) Pre-publish checklist

  1. Consent proof is stored and accessible.
  2. The output is not misleading (no impersonation, no false endorsement).
  3. Audio levels are consistent (no clipping, minimal background noise).
  4. Subtitles match the final audio and contain no sensitive information.

FAQ

  • Can I clone someone else’s voice? Only with explicit permission. Without consent, don’t do it.
  • Should I disclose AI voice use? In many contexts (ads, support), it’s recommended for trust.
  • How do I avoid a “robotic” result? Improve the script, segment it, and tweak punctuation/pacing before export.
